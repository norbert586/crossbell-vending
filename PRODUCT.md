## Users

The primary audience is businesses considering hosting a machine. Workplaces and shared spaces need refreshments; family venues and retail locations may also host trading-card vending.

## Product Purpose

Help a prospective host understand Crossbell's managed vending services and request a site visit. Crossbell operates in Macomb and north Oakland County, Michigan.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Smart coolers, secure multi-cabinet smart markets, and trading-card vending.

- Placement is subject to site suitability. Hosts provide appropriate indoor space, power, and service access. Connectivity and mounting are assessed during the visit.
- Preserve Netlify Forms, QR routing, lead attribution, existing cooler model links, legal pages, and resident promotions.
- Do not invent placements, testimonials, sales projections, guaranteed availability, official Pokémon affiliation, or host income.
