Crossbell is a local, hands-on vending operator serving Macomb and northern Oakland County. We assess your space, install suitable smart vending equipment, handle product sourcing, routine restocking, payment technology and ongoing support. Your team does not have to manage inventory or collect payments.

For qualifying locations, placement and ongoing machine service are provided at no charge to the host. We simply assess space, electrical access and expected usage before placement.
