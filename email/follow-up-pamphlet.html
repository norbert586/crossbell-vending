No long-term lock-in. After the first 90 days, thirty days' written notice ends it. Because we purchase, stock, and install each cooler at our own expense, an early-exit fee applies inside those first 90 days — spelled out in the agreement before you sign.
What if something goes wrong with it?
Call or message us and you hear back the same business day. Refunds are handled by us, not by your staff, and a replacement unit can be on site within 48 hours.
